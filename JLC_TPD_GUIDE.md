# ⌚ Jaeger-LeCoultre (JLC) TPD & Winding Specifications Guide

**Jaeger-LeCoultre**, known as the "Watchmaker of Watchmakers," is a manufacture defined by technical mastery. From the rotatable **Reverso** to the high-precision **Master Ultra Thin**, JLC calibers are engineered to the most exacting standards, requiring optimized winding cycles to maintain their 1,000 Hours Control certification.

## 🛠 The "Watchmaker of Watchmakers" Standard
Jaeger-LeCoultre movements are celebrated for their thin profiles and mechanical efficiency. However, their precision—especially in the **Master Control** and **Duomètre** lines—is sensitive to the magnetic environments of modern life. A professional winding environment is not just a convenience; it is a technical necessity.

| JLC Series / Collection | Movement (Calibre) | TPD Setting | Direction |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Reverso Tribute (Auto)** | Cal. 965 / 967 | 650 | Both (Bi-directional) |
| **Master Ultra Thin** | Cal. 899 / 925 | 650 | Both (Bi-directional) |
| **Master Control Date** | Cal. 899 | 650 | Both (Bi-directional) |
| **Polaris Automatic** | Cal. 898 | 650 - 800 | Both (Bi-directional) |
| **Rendez-Vous** | Cal. 898 / 925 | 650 | Both (Bi-directional) |
| **Master Compressor** | Cal. 751 (Chrono) | 800 | Both (Bi-directional) |

> **💡 Technical Note:** Most modern JLC automatic calibers utilize a bi-directional winding system that performs optimally at **650 TPD**. For vintage JLC models or specific complication calibers, always verify the winding direction to ensure the vertical clutch remains engaged.
