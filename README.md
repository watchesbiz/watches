## 🔍 What is TPD?
Turns Per Day (TPD) is the specific number of rotations required to keep an automatic watch movement fully wound. Incorrect TPD settings or winding directions (Clockwise, Counter-clockwise, or Both) can lead to insufficient power reserves or unnecessary mechanical wear.

## 🔍 Why TPD Matters
Incorrect winding can lead to accuracy drift or mechanical wear. Most luxury timepieces require between **650 and 950 TPD**. Using an unshielded winder can also expose your movement to **Stray Magnetic Fields**, the silent killer of hairspring precision.
