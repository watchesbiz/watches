# ⌚ Rolex Watch TPD & Winding Specifications Guide

This technical guide provides the optimal **Turns Per Day (TPD)** settings and winding directions for the most iconic **Rolex** calibers. Correct winding ensures the longevity of the perpetual movement and maintains chronometer-level precision.

## 🛠 Why Rolex Calibers Require Precise Winding
Rolex movements, such as the **Calibre 3235** or **4130**, are engineered for high efficiency. Over-winding or using a magnetic winder can compromise the balance spring's hair-thin tolerances.

| Rolex Model / Series | Movement (Calibre) | TPD Setting | Direction |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Daytona** | Cal. 4130 / 4131 | 650 - 800 | Both (Bi-directional) |
| **Submariner Date** | Cal. 3135 / 3235 | 650 | Both (Bi-directional) |

| **Datejust 36/41** | Cal. 3235 | 650 | Both (Bi-directional) |

> **Note:** Most modern Rolex Perpetual movements perform optimally at **650 TPD** with bi-directional rotation.
