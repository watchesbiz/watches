# ⌚ Panerai (OFFICINE PANERAI) TPD & Winding Specifications Guide

**Panerai**, with its roots in the Royal Italian Navy, creates instruments of extreme robustness. From the iconic **Luminor** bridge to the elegant **Radiomir**, maintaining the high torque required for their long-power-reserve **P.XXXX** calibers is essential for peak performance.

## 🛠 Engineering Challenge: High-Torque Calibers
Many Panerai movements, such as the **P.9010** or the 10-day **P.2003**, feature dual or triple barrels to provide extended power. These require consistent winding to ensure the constant force escapement maintains chronometric precision throughout the power curve.

## 📊 Panerai TPD Data Table (Key Calibers)

| Panerai Series / Collection | Movement (Calibre) | TPD Setting | Direction |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Luminor Marina** | Cal. P.9010 / P.900 | 800 | Clockwise (CW) |
| **Luminor GMT (10-Day)** | Cal. P.2003 | 800 | Clockwise (CW) |
| **Radiomir** | Cal. P.4000 (Micro-rotor) | 800 | Both (Bi-directional) |
| **Submersible** | Cal. OP XXXIV / P.900 | 800 | Clockwise (CW) |
| **Luminor Due** | Cal. P.900 | 800 | Clockwise (CW) |
| **Luminor (ETA-based)** | Cal. OP III / OP VIII | 650 - 800 | Both (Bi-directional) |

> **⚠️ Critical Note:** A significant number of in-house Panerai calibers (like the P.9000 series) are **Uni-directional (Clockwise)**. Setting your winder to "Both" or "CCW" may not provide sufficient tension for these professional diving instruments.
