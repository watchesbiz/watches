# ⌚ Vacheron Constantin (VC) TPD & Winding Specifications Guide

**Vacheron Constantin**, the world's oldest watch manufacture in continuous operation since 1755, represents the pinnacle of "Belle Haute Horlogerie." To preserve the chronometric excellence of movements bearing the **Hallmark of Geneva (Poinçon de Genève)**, precise winding parameters are indispensable.

## 🛠 Engineering Excellence: Beyond the Cross
From the ultra-thin **Calibre 1120** to the modern **Calibre 5100** found in the **Overseas** collection, Vacheron Constantin movements are masterpieces of friction reduction and finishing. Using a winder with inferior shielding can introduce magnetic micro-stresses to the delicate hairspring, compromising the very precision the Geneva Seal guarantees.

## 📊 Vacheron Constantin TPD Data Table (Core Collections)

| VC Series / Collection | Movement (Calibre) | TPD Setting | Direction |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Overseas Self-Winding** | Cal. 5100 | 650 - 800 | Both (Bi-directional) |
| **Overseas Dual Time** | Cal. 5110 DT | 650 - 800 | Both (Bi-directional) |
| **Patrimony Self-Winding** | Cal. 2450 QPG | 650 | Both (Bi-directional) |
| **Traditionnelle** | Cal. 2455 | 650 | Both (Bi-directional) |
| **Historiques Ultra-Fine** | Cal. 1120 | 650 | Both (Bi-directional) |
| **FiftySix Automatic** | Cal. 1326 | 650 | Both (Bi-directional) |

> **💡 Technical Note:** While most modern VC calibers are bi-directional, their high-complication movements (Perpetual Calendars) require extremely stable TPD cycles to ensure the calendar-switching mechanism receives constant, uniform torque.
